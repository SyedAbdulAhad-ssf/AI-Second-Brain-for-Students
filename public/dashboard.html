BEST UPLOAD PRACTICES

How to Upload PDFs for the Best Results

01 · Ideal size: 10 to 25 pages

Upload one single chapter or topic at a time instead of an entire 200-page textbook. This helps the AI focus on formulas, key points, and diagrams.

02 · Scanned and digital PDFs supported

Searchable text PDFs and scanned textbook image PDFs, including Sindh Board, Matric, and Intermediate chapters, are supported through built-in OCR and Vision extraction.

03 · Tag your subject

Enter the subject (such as Chemistry, Physics, or Computer Science) and chapter title to keep saved notes organized.

QUICK HELP

Common questions

Why did a full 150-page book give a generic summary, while one chapter gave a better result?

A single 10–25 page chapter lets the AI focus on formulas, definitions, and subtopics. Very large books may exceed the amount of detail the analysis can handle at once.

How do I switch between subjects?

Select a saved note in Smart Notes, Quiz, or Chat. Your active workspace context syncs automatically.
